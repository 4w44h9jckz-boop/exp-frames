  new run): a lost device, not a stolen one. A thief holding the owner's key can cancel every
  recovery, as the owner cancelled the hostile guardians', and the seed on the device gives it

### Learned

- **W14, hardening.** Not every path in experiment 45 is hardened. The owner keys sit on BIP-44's
  `m/44'/60'/0'/0/<i>`, whose last two levels are not: an extended public key at
  `m/44'/60'/0'/0` would link every owner key, and with one owner's private key would give the
  parent. The wallet never publishes that key, and experiment 45's observation 6 says so. Every
  HKDF label is a one-way function of the seed, with no public counterpart to link. A derivation
  that is hardened throughout, as the owner's note asks, would move the owners to a fully
  hardened path, at the cost of BIP-44 compatibility with other wallets.
- **W17,** recorded for its plan: the binding covers the whole signature hash, the fee fields and
  the payer included; and if M11's one-time scheme is hash-based, the wallet never signs a
  replacement transaction with that key.
