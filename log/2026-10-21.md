# 2026-10-21

## The first wallet session, phase 4: W11's first question, a Noir transfer's verifier in `VERIFY`

(Experiment 41's entry for the same day is on branch `exp/41-fhe-input-proof`. Whichever merges
second carries both entries.)

### Done

- **Experiment 42** (branch `exp/42-noir-transfer`): pre-registered in `6829a95` before any run;
  the first results in `86e3ceb`.
  - The circuit is a transfer on Aztec's note and nullifier model: two notes spent from a depth-32
    tree, two created, Poseidon2 throughout, seven public inputs. It was compiled with Noir
    1.0.0-rc.6 and proved with `bb.js` 5.2.1, both unmodified (MIT OR Apache-2.0, and MIT). The
    zero-knowledge proof takes 1.1 s and is 8,000 B; the proof without zero knowledge takes 0.5 s
    and is 7,072 B.
  - Barretenberg's two Solidity verifiers were deployed on the testnet, with their libraries linked.
    Both accept the real proof and refuse it once a public input is changed. With zero knowledge
    the verifier costs 3,494,968 gas; without it, 1,782,522. That is 7.0 and 3.6 times the
    testnet's 500,000, and 35 and 18 times EIP-8141's 100,000.
  - `src/contracts.ts` now returns a contract's link references and links library addresses
    (`linkBytecode`). Tests: 1,183 passing.

### Learned

- No pairing-based proof fits EIP-8141's `VERIFY` budget on the EVM. Two pairs already cost 113,000
  on BN254 (EIP-1108) and 102,900 on BLS12-381 (EIP-2537). For X4 that settles the 100,000 budget
  by the schedule, whatever proof system Noir compiles to.
- Groth16 over BN254 is the one candidate left for the testnet's 500,000. Its floor is 224,050
  gas at seven public inputs; that is projected, not measured. It costs a trusted setup per
  circuit.
- Reilabs' Sunspot (Apache-2.0, audited; the Go module `github.com/reilabs/sunspot/go` at
  `0a9f7d01`) compiles Noir's ACIR to gnark's Groth16. It built here from the Go module proxy.
  It has not been run, for two reasons. It is not among the third-party pieces the phase's rule
  names. And this environment refused to execute a binary built from downloaded code without a
  person's say.
