### Learned

- **Done when: met. Would show it wrong: not observed, with one condition.** A ticket's signature
  and a note someone else made cannot be derived from the seed. They came back from the chain
  because the wallet had put them there, the answer blinded and the opening sealed to the viewing

- A deterministic blinding factor tells the committee nothing: to anyone without the seed, the
  request is a uniformly random point. It makes a repeated request identical, which is what lets
  a wallet ask again after a loss without being counted twice.
- The answer post shows everyone what only the issuers saw before: that the buyer was issued two
  tickets in one epoch.
- One leaked seed opens all of it, including every ticket's link to its purchase and every
  announcement ever sealed to the viewing key.

### Done

- **Experiment 46** (branch `exp/46-guardians`, stacked on `exp/45-one-seed`): the claim and the
  plan pushed before any run in `76eda6d`; the run, its record, eighteen fixtures, the read-back
  and the results in `7cc929a`.
  - Guardians hold Shamir 2-of-3 shares of a fresh recovery key `R`, never of the seed. `R` is the
    one device of experiment 31's account, unchanged, behind its 120 s delay.
  - The seed's envelope is posted to the board from a ticket-paid fresh sender, under `R` and an
    Argon2id-stretched phrase the user keeps.
  - Setup (490,681 to 490,714): commitment to `R0`; two guardians told the account and salt
    start a recovery (`0x723f22ff…`), the owner cancels (`0x9fa99861…`), their finish is refused;
    rotation to `R1` (`0x37f39d70…`); the old set's start refused at admission.
  - Recovery with no seed file and no funder key (490,717 to 490,743): two guardians post their
    shares sealed to the new device; it joins `R1`, opens the envelope with the phrase, `R1`
    starts a recovery to owner 1 (`0x09df2934…`), owner 1 finishes after the delay
    (`0x0a47dc4f…`) and rotates to `R2`, posting its envelope with a ticket rebuilt from the
    board.
- **kohaku-rs `adecefb`** (branch `experiment/secrets`): the `guardians` module and example,
  `tests/record46.rs`; `seed_wallet`'s restore now accepts any of the seed's first owners, which
  X7 needs after a recovery.
- Suite on `exp/46-guardians`: 1,309 of 1,309; `tsc` clean; `scripts/check_private_names.py` clean.

### Learned

- **Done when: met.** **Would show it wrong: observed for one construction, and in part for two
  clauses.** A delay binds only what the chain enforces. An envelope that `R` alone opens gave two
  guardians the seed at once, and the wallet's notes with no delay; the phrase is what keeps
  them out. Rotation takes back the account, but not an envelope already published: the old set
  with the phrase opened the first envelope after the recovery. And guardians hold their shares
  from setup to rotation, which is longer than any recovery; rotation bounds that, nothing
  removes it.
- The salt in the commitment is a second lock: a quorum without the seed cannot reveal the
  commitment, so cannot start a recovery.
- Shares posted on the board under a public tag, two blocks before the start, tie those
  guardians to the account by timing, and their ECDH sealing is not post-quantum.
- An account deployed with an empty commitment pays 97,920 state gas for its first one.

### For the owner

- A choice this entry made that the register's claim did not: the envelope needs a phrase the
  user keeps besides the guardians' key. Without it the claim fails, as the run shows. If the
  owner prefers the claim tested without any user factor, that construction is in the code and
  the record (`seal(.., None, ..)`), and its result is the failure above.

- Suite on `exp/47-backup`: 1,330 of 1,330; `tsc` clean; `scripts/check_private_names.py` clean.

### Learned

- **Done when: met. Would show it wrong: observed in this run, through the payer and the timing,
  not through what was posted.** The posts are random bytes from fresh senders, but the ticket
  paymaster that paid for them has one buyer, owner 0, and version 0 followed owner 0's answer
  post by two blocks. A ticket hides its buyer only among its paymaster's buyers. The sizes fell
  into two buckets, and a backup's length is unlike any other message on the board.
- **One leaked key:** one version's key opens that version only, but versions are cumulative,
  so the newest key opens the whole history and a received note's opening that anyone holding
  it can spend. The seed opens all. Experiment 46's first guardian set with the phrase still
  opens the seed through the first envelope, so both versions too.
- The guardians' key does not open the backup: it opens the envelope, the envelope the seed, the
  seed each version's key.
