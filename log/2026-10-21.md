### Done

- **Experiment 42** (branch `exp/42-noir-transfer`): pre-registered in `6829a95` before any run;
  the first results in `86e3ceb`.
  - The circuit is a transfer on Aztec's note and nullifier model: two notes spent from a depth-32
    tree, two created, Poseidon2 throughout, seven public inputs. It was compiled with Noir
    1.0.0-rc.6 and proved with `bb.js` 5.2.1, both unmodified (MIT OR Apache-2.0, and MIT). The
    zero-knowledge proof takes 1.1 s and is 8,000 B; the proof without zero knowledge takes 0.5 s
    and is 7,072 B.
  - Barretenberg's two Solidity verifiers were deployed on the testnet, with their libraries linked.
    Both accept the real proof and refuse it once a public input is changed. With zero knowledge
    the verifier costs 3,494,968 gas; without it, 1,782,522. That is 7.0 and 3.6 times the
    testnet's 500,000, and 35 and 18 times EIP-8141's 100,000.
  - `src/contracts.ts` now returns a contract's link references and links library addresses
    (`linkBytecode`). Tests: 1,183 passing.

- The transfer as a frame transaction (`pool.ts`, `pool.json`):
  - `Poseidon2.yul` was generated from Barretenberg's parameter file, which is copied with
    attribution under `vendor/`. It matches Barretenberg's outputs.
  - `NotePool.yul` keeps the depth-32 note tree on L1 and files its roots with EIP-8272. The two
    nullifiers are the transfer's EIP-8250 nonce keys.
  - With Barretenberg's zero-knowledge verifier, the testnet refused the transfer twice: by
    MAX_VERIFY_GAS at the limit it needs, and by a `VERIFY` revert at 490,000.
  - With `AcceptAnyProof` in the verifier's place, the same transaction was mined in block 491,124,
    and a second spend of the same notes was refused by the nonce keys. That run is labelled a
    layout check, not a pool.
  - Barretenberg accepts both pools' proofs by call.

- **kohaku-rs** (branch `experiment/noir-transfer`, `c1564b9`, crate `crates/noir-transfer`):
  the transfer proved from Rust. Noir's executor (`acvm` 1.0.0-rc.6) builds the witness, and
  `barretenberg-rs` 5.2.1 drives Barretenberg's `bb` 5.2.1 to prove it, all unmodified from
  crates.io and npm. Its Poseidon2 and witness match the JavaScript packages.
  - A second layout transfer, proved this way, was mined in block 491,204. Barretenberg's verifier
    accepts its proof by call.

### Learned

- No pairing-based proof fits EIP-8141's `VERIFY` budget on the EVM. Two pairs already cost 113,000
  on BN254 (EIP-1108) and 102,900 on BLS12-381 (EIP-2537). For X4 that settles the 100,000 budget
  by the schedule, whatever proof system Noir compiles to.
- The verifier is 99.7% of the prefix a real transfer needs: 3,506,745 gas in all. Everything
  else (the root frame and the pool's checks) costs 11,777 gas and fits both budgets.
- Without the rollup, the note tree costs about 1.0M gas a note on L1 at depth 32 with Poseidon2:
  2,337,257 for a transfer's two new notes. That is execution, not validation, so no budget
  refuses it, but it is what a transfer costs.
- The verifier without zero knowledge is half the gas, but it is no option for a private transfer:
  its proof is not zero-knowledge.
- Noir's executor and Barretenberg's Rust bindings are both on crates.io at the pinned releases,
  so a Rust wallet can prove a Noir circuit with no fork. The one thing it cannot do is compile:
  the compiler is not published, so the compiled circuit is a fixture.
- Groth16 over BN254 is the one candidate left for the testnet's 500,000. Its floor is 224,050
  gas at seven public inputs; that is projected, not measured. It costs a trusted setup per
  circuit.
- Reilabs' Sunspot (Apache-2.0, audited; the Go module `github.com/reilabs/sunspot/go` at
  `0a9f7d01`) compiles Noir's ACIR to gnark's Groth16. It built here from the Go module proxy.
  It has not been run, for two reasons. It is not among the third-party pieces the phase's rule
  names. And this environment refused to execute a binary built from downloaded code without a
  person's say.
