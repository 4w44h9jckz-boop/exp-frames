## The first wallet session, later: the owner's answer on X5's input side

### Done

- The owner chose (a): X5's input side is recorded as failing on experiment 41's measurement. The
  result side, the committee's threshold signature checked in `VERIFY`, is untouched and keeps
  running. No SNARK of the encryption relation, and no off-chain check by the committee.

### Learned

- **Where the floor's 504 gas per permutation comes from.** The coordinator asked where the floor's
  per-permutation price comes from, since a sibling measured about 41,587 gas for a Keccak-f
  permutation in EVM code.
  - The 504 is not a measurement, and no implementation is expected to reach it. It is a floor:
    $\chi$, Keccak-f's only nonlinear step, needs at least seven 256-bit bitwise operations
    (`AND`, `OR` or `NOT`) per round on 64-bit lanes packed four to a word. At 3 gas each, over
    24 rounds, that is $7 \cdot 3 \cdot 24 = 504$.
  - It leaves out $\theta$, $\rho$, $\pi$, $\iota$, the lane packing, memory and control flow,
    so any real permutation costs more. The sibling's 41,587 is one such real cost.
  - Priced at 41,587, the transcript alone is $4{,}223 \cdot 41{,}587 \approx 175.6$ million gas
    for one block at $d = 2048$, which agrees with the sibling's 176 million.
  - The floor used the lower bound so that the verdict cannot be argued down by a better Keccak.
    Either price gives the same verdict: it does not fit, by about a factor of 8 at the floor and
    about 350 at the measured price. The README's section "Divergences from the plan" already says
    the permutation was priced by the $\chi$ argument instead of a measurement.
