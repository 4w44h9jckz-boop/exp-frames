### Learned

- **Done when: met. Would show it wrong: not observed, with one condition.** A ticket's signature
  and a note someone else made cannot be derived from the seed. They came back from the chain
  because the wallet had put them there, the answer blinded and the opening sealed to the viewing
  key. The claim holds as a rule the wallet keeps, not as a property of derivation alone.
- A deterministic blinding factor tells the committee nothing: to anyone without the seed, the
  request is a uniformly random point. It makes a repeated request identical, which is what lets
  a wallet ask again after a loss without being counted twice.
- The answer post shows everyone what only the issuers saw before: that the buyer was issued two
  tickets in one epoch.
- One leaked seed opens all of it, including every ticket's link to its purchase and every
  announcement ever sealed to the viewing key.
