### Learned

- Self-deployment caps a fresh sender's code at 206 bytes. A one-key leanSPHINCS account fits,
  but its first transaction costs about twice an EOA's.
- Without a chain that checks the dependency, a ticket bound by a leanSPHINCS key binds nothing.
  Every refusal of a raised-fee copy has to come from the block's proof, or from a mempool that
  checks the witness.
