### Done

- **F4, one registry for two applications** (this repository `88ad0be`, kohaku-rs `3e95bfc`).
  Experiment 32's `OwnedStateRegistry` is its own sender and holds the notes of every
  application in one MiMC tree. A note is $H(\mathrm{tag}, \mathrm{inner}, \mathrm{state})$ and
  its nullifier $H(\mathrm{tag}, \mathrm{nullifierSecret})$. A kernel proof (`kernel.circom`,
  33,287 constraints) is checked in `VERIFY`, with the nullifier as the nonce key and the root
  through EIP-8272. Two applications, `FixedPool` (experiment 06's pool as a rule) and
  `Balance` (any amount, partial spends with a change note), and a control, `StoredRule`.
  Run once on the testnet (`npx tsx experiments/32-owned-state/run.ts`):
  - **Both applications write to one registry, and both self-updates were mined.** The same
    secrets, deposited in both, gave two nullifiers: A (`0x511572f1`, block 479,289, FixedPool)
    and B (`0xa5b18e15`, block 479,291, Balance with a 0.002 ETH change note). C (`0x587e541e`,
    block 479,292) spent the change note.
  - **A nullifier from one application is refused by the other.** A's proof with Balance's tag
    was refused before A (D1); the prover cannot make a Balance update with FixedPool's
    nullifier (D2); after A, its nullifier is refused whichever application claims it (D4).
  - **The check needs no storage outside the sender.** The registry's `VERIFY` reads no
    storage. The control, whose rule reads one slot of its own contract, was refused at
    admission with `StorageReadNonSender` (E3).
  - `VERIFY` costs 229,302 to 229,326 gas per update, over EIP-8141's 100,000 and under the
    testnet's 500,000, and 15,494 less than experiment 06's keyed pool.
  - kohaku-rs's new `crates/frame-registry` (`kohaku-frame-registry`) rebuilds the three updates
    byte for byte from the fixtures, verifies their proofs with its own Groth16 check, recomputes
    the deposits' leaves and the tree's roots with `kohaku-tornadocash`'s MiMC (now exported as
    `mimc_sponge`), and matches `registry.ts`'s kernel input for a spend signal by signal.

### Learned

- **A shared tree is not a shared crowd.** The tag is public, so an update's anonymity set is
  its application's notes. The registry shares a root publication, a verifier and a nonce-key
  space, not privacy; hiding the tag needs the application's rule inside the proof.
- **What an application may check in `VERIFY` is code only.** State it keeps in its own
  contract has to move into the note, the proof or the registry's storage.
- **A change note costs a deposit**: 977,066 gas through experiment 06's hasher contract.
- The phase-2 setup has one contribution, made here with discarded entropy; the proving key is
  committed so the deployed verifier stays usable (README, "The proving key"). Phase 1 is the
  PSE perpetual powers of tau; Hermez's file returned 403.
