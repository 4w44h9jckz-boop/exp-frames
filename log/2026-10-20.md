### Learned

- **ethrex's `--dev` mode cannot produce a Hegota block, on any branch.** Its block producer sends
  `engine_forkchoiceUpdatedV4` for every fork from Amsterdam on, and ethrex refuses Hegota payload
  attributes on V4, so the node shuts down after three tries. That holds on `hegota-testnet`,
  `hegota-testnet-hotfix`, `hegota-devnet` and `main` as of 2026-10-19. F0 drives the engine API
  itself instead and runs the stock binary.
- **The three predeploys are installed by the client, not the genesis.** The testnet's published
  `genesis.json` has no code at `0x...8141`, `0x...8250` or `0x...8272`. ethrex installs all three
  in the first Hegota block, and on the local node they are byte for byte the testnet's.
- **`ethrex_simulateFrameTransaction` needs `--http.api ...,ethrex`.** The default namespaces are
  `eth,net,web3`.
- **Rust and TypeScript price the refund withdrawal identically.** Given the same simulation and
  fees, both chose 11,973 / 310,930 / 100,000 / 25,000 and a fee of 150,658,200,000,000 wei. The
  only difference on chain is calldata: 36 gas, from three fewer zero bytes.

### For X1, the command that builds and sends the withdrawal

From kohaku-rs `claude/nifty-einstein-llp9cd` at `2836329`:

```text
PRIVATE_KEY=0x... SEND_RPC_URL=<the first skandha node> \
  cargo run --release -p kohaku-frame-pool --example refund_withdrawal
```

It deposits a fresh note through `RPC_URL` (the testnet by default), prices the withdrawal
against it, hands the withdrawal to `SEND_RPC_URL` alone with `eth_sendRawTransaction`, and waits
for the receipt on `RPC_URL`. Tried with both URLs at the testnet: `0x07f534f4`, block 478,011.
